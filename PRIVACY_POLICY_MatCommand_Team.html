Privacy Policy – MatCommand Team

Effective Date: May 12, 2026

MatCommand Team respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored and used within the application.

Information Collected

MatCommand Team may store information entered by coaches, team administrators, or users, including wrestler names, birthdays, weight classes, profile pictures, parent and emergency contact information, attendance records, apparel sizing information, volunteer information, and medical or safety notes entered by team staff.

How Information Is Used

The information entered into MatCommand Team is used only for team management and organizational purposes, including attendance tracking, team roster management, volunteer coordination, apparel sizing and reporting, emergency contact reference, team operations, and scheduling.

Data Storage

At this time, MatCommand Team primarily stores data locally on the user's device. Backups and exported files created by the user are stored only where the user chooses to save them. MatCommand Team does not sell user data.

Data Sharing

MatCommand Team does not share personal information with third parties. Any exported files or reports are controlled entirely by the user or organization operating the app.

Photos and Media

Profile pictures added within the app are stored locally as part of the app data and backups unless exported by the user.

Security

Reasonable efforts are made to keep app data secure. However, users are responsible for securing their own devices, backups, and exported files.

Children's Privacy

MatCommand Team is intended for use by coaches, clubs, schools, and team administrators. The app is not directed toward children for independent use.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time. Future updates will be reflected within the app listing or associated distribution channels.
